## 中文速读

**一句话**：Goal2Pixel 将 VLN-CE 从低层动作预测改写为可导航像素 grounding，让 VLM 预测图像中的可行进像素并反投影为 3D 路标，从而减少 VLM 调用并提升导航表现。 \
**为什么相关**：高度相关于具身智能导航、VLN-CE、机器人导航、目标到空间位置的视觉 grounding，以及从图像平面到 3D waypoint 的空间推理。 \
**研究问题**：现有基于 VLM 的 VLN-CE 方法多将导航建模为低层动作预测，接口含糊、依赖短视野运动原语，并因反复查询 VLM 而效率较低。 \
**方法要点**：提出纯像素范式 Goal2Pixel，用图像平面作为 VLM 推理和机器人运动的统一空间接口；模型预测可见可导航像素，并反投影为 3D waypoint；左、右、底部辅助区域分别表示左转、右转和停止；使用可见性感知关键帧记忆表示历史，并加入语义嵌入和坐标感知辅助损失适配预训练 VLM。 \
**实验/结果**：在 R2R-CE Val-Unseen 上达到 54.1% SR 和 52.5% SPL，每个 episode 仅需 7.75 次 VLM 调用；相比直接动作预测的 32.9% SR 和 46.62 次调用，调用次数减少约 6 倍；摘要称 RxR-CE 上也有相同趋势。 \
**阅读判断**：建议关注其将语言目标落地到图像像素并转为 3D waypoint 的接口设计，以及关键帧记忆如何支持长程 VLN-CE；对研究 VLN、GoalNav、ObjectNav 中的空间 grounding 和高效 VLM 调用有参考价值。

## 英文摘要原文

Vision-language models (VLMs) have become a common foundation for vision-and-language navigation in continuous environments (VLN-CE). Yet most VLM-based methods cast navigation as low-level action prediction, an interface that is ambiguous, tied to short-horizon motion primitives, and inefficient due to repeated VLM querying. We propose Goal2Pixel, a pure pixel-based paradigm that reformulates VLN-CE as navigable pixel grounding. Rather than predicting actions, Goal2Pixel uses the image plane as a unified spatial interface between VLM reasoning and robot motion: the model predicts a visible navigable pixel to the agent, which is back-projected into a 3D waypoint for forward navigation. For non-forward actions, we append auxiliary directive regions to the image plane, where the left/right/bottom regions are interpreted as turning left, turning right, and stopping, respectively. To enable long-horizon navigation, we propose a visibility-aware keyframe memory for compact and informative history representation. To adapt pretrained VLMs to navigable pixel grounding, we introduce semantic embeddings and coordinate-aware auxiliary losses. Goal2Pixel achieves competitive state-of-the-art performance while requiring fewer VLM inference calls than prior methods. On R2R-CE Val-Unseen it achieves 54.1% SR and 52.5% SPL with just 7.75 VLM calls per episode, 6x fewer than the 46.62 required by direct action prediction at 32.9% SR.
